# Session Reflection 2 — 2026-07-26 (ruff 0.16, CI feedback, WI-028 lockfile)

**Work summary:** After the security-fix sweep, three more commits: adopted ruff 0.16 with a deliberate explicit rule select (WI-028, `59c25f1`); applied the CI-reduction feedback — bumped checkout/setup-python to node24 SHAs and made the identifier-gate path assertions separator-agnostic (`fb6bba6`); and wired CI to the committed `uv.lock` via `uv sync --frozen` so an upstream release can no longer redden main (`cf18e9e`).

---

## On the project

The lockfile change was the real lever behind the whole "ruff broke main" failure class — and the sharpest moment was discovering the `uv.lock` had silently drifted to ruff 0.15.20 after the pin was bumped without a refresh (and that the lock was gitignored, making a frozen resolve impossible from a fresh checkout). Both are exactly the kind of latent gap that a focused pass surfaces and a scattered one misses. The deliberate-select decision (E4/E7/E9 not full-E, excluding TRY/BLE/PLW/SIM/S) is defensible and matches the family, but it does leave agent-suite/agent-wake/dossier on a narrower E than regista/cairn — the E501 backlog is the visible consequence.

## On the work done

Confident in: `uv lock --check` passes, `uv sync --frozen --extra dev` installs exactly ruff 0.16.0, and the full CI lint command runs green through `uv run` locally (so CI parity is proven, not assumed). The `--frozen` (not `--locked`) choice is deliberate: `--locked` re-resolves and would red precisely when an upstream release drops — the opposite of the goal.

Less confident / caveats: I rewrote the whole `ci.yml` rather than surgical edits — higher chance of a typo slipping through; I YAML-parsed it and ran the `uv run` invocations, but the interop/feature-probes jobs (sibling `uv pip install` into the venv) are unexercised locally (they need Postgres + network). The windows-test job now relies on `uv run` resolving `.venv/Scripts` on Windows — not validated on a Windows runner here.

## Gaps to flag

A `uv lock --check` step would catch it, but that re-resolves and reintroduces upstream-release red risk — the tradeoff is deliberate.
- **`.venv/` is created by `uv run`/`uv venv` locally** — confirmed gitignored, but the ruff016 throwaway venv at `/tmp/opencode/ruff016` and the test venv at `/tmp/opencode/as-venv-test` are outside the repo (cleanup-safe).
