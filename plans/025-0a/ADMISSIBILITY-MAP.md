# ADMISSIBILITY-MAP - 0A-4 internal draft, 2026-08-25

This map is an engineering reading of adjacent control and evidence paradigms, not a legal, audit, or compliance opinion. Isomorphism here is a design hypothesis, not evidence that a regulator, auditor, relying party, or target environment accepts a claim. Under the D3 stage-1 posture, every entry has validation status `internal-hypothesis`; no production acceptance may rest on this map until the target environment's risk/compliance/control owner performs D3 stage 2 validation. Claim names are provisional until 0C.

`ASSUMPTION:` The claim fields and adversary limits in `TRUST-MODEL.md` section 7, the common claim-field guarantees in `INV-019` through `INV-023`, and the residuals in section 12 remain controlling. An analogy cannot enlarge a claim's declared scope or adversary resistance.

## Citation posture

The citations below intentionally use only clauses or named data concepts that this engineering draft can identify confidently:

- HIPAA Security Rule, 45 CFR 164.312(a)(1), Access Control; 164.312(b), Audit Controls; 164.312(c)(1), Integrity; 164.312(d), Person or Entity Authentication; and 164.312(e)(1), Transmission Security. These provisions state safeguard objectives; they do not prescribe this evidence format or establish its admissibility.
- SLSA Provenance v1.0 uses an in-toto Statement v1 with `subject` name/digest and a provenance predicate containing `buildDefinition` and `runDetails`. The in-toto Statement v1 binds a `_type`, `subject`, `predicateType`, and `predicate`. Sigstore bundles package verification material for offline verification. These are supply-chain evidence concepts, not agent-governance approval criteria.
- AICPA Trust Services Criteria CC8.1 addresses authorization, design/development or acquisition, configuration, documentation, testing, approval, and implementation of changes. `VERIFY:` The target reviewer must identify the exact ITGC or SOC 2 criteria and control wording it uses for segregation of duties and independent approval; this map does not treat “SOC 2” as one universal evidence prescription.
- RFC 3161 section 2.1 defines TSA requirements; sections 2.4.1 and 2.4.2 define the timestamp request and response. The `TSTInfo` structure in section 2.4.2 binds, among other fields, policy, `messageImprint`, serial number, and `genTime`, with optional accuracy and nonce-related processing.
- Forensic chain of custody is used here as the general practice of uniquely identifying an item, recording each transfer or handling action, preserving chronological continuity and integrity, and documenting gaps. `VERIFY:` There is no single chain-of-custody clause claimed by this draft; the target reviewer must name its governing procedure, standard, and required custodian record.

## Claim hypotheses

### `IndependentReviewAttestation`

- **Hypothesised paradigm(s):** ITGC / SOC 2 change-management and segregation-of-duties records; forensic chain of custody for identity and continuity of the reviewed subject.
- **Borrowed clause/concept:** AICPA TSC CC8.1's authorization, testing, approval, and implementation-of-change concepts; organizational separation between proposer and authorized approver; unique subject identification and an unbroken record from reviewed bytes to admitted action. `VERIFY:` The target's precise SoD criterion, role model, conflict rules, approval evidence, and whether CC8.1 is the applicable SOC 2 mapping.
- **Analogy-breaking failure mode:** Different model lineage is treated as segregation of duties despite the same person, account, authority chain, incentives, or unresolved conflict controlling both roles; approval is a free-text name or detached verdict; the human saw different bytes from the bound subject; or the gate admits a different action. A cryptographic signature alone does not prove independence, comprehension, or human intent (`INV-026` through `INV-029`, `INV-041`; ADV-06, ADV-10, ADV-17, ADV-20, ADV-24, ADV-28; `TRUST-MODEL.md` section 12).
- **Fields required for the analogy:** All common section 7 fields, especially subject digest, authority-policy id/version, verification cut, separate Q1/Q2/Q3 results, completeness scope, warnings, and verifier version; plus reviewer principal/key, authenticated human identity, role, organizational authority, lineage, conflict and independence declarations and evaluated result, reviewed subject digest/range, review verdict, human-approval identity, and gate decision/action id. The approval must bind the exact rendered subject and policy context (`INV-019` through `INV-023`, `INV-026`, `INV-028`, `INV-041`).
- **Independence policy:** The policy must authenticate the reviewer as a distinct principal and human identity; require a reviewer role authorized by the named organization for the subject and action; establish that the role has approval authority at the recorded policy version and cut; prohibit the author/requester, subject signer, action implementer, and anyone with a declared financial, supervisory, operational, or other policy-defined conflict from satisfying the independent role; evaluate required organizational separation rather than lineage alone; bind the exact subject digest/range and requested action; record the policy id/version and independence result; require an explicit human approval bound to the rendered subject digest; and deny on absent, unknown, stale, contradictory, or unverifiable identity, authority, conflict, subject, or approval data. Lineage diversity may be an additional review-quality rule but never substitutes for organizational independence (`INV-022`, `INV-025` through `INV-028`, `INV-041`). `OPEN:` The owner must designate the authoritative identity source, organizational authority source, minimum separation, conflict categories, recusal procedure, and any dual-approval roles.
- **Validation status:** `internal-hypothesis`.
- **D3 stage-2 question:** Does the target control owner regard this identity/authority/conflict policy and subject-to-action binding as sufficient evidence of independent review and approval for its actual change-control and SoD control, and what roles, exceptions, retention, or re-performance evidence are missing?

### `TamperEvidentChangeRecord`

- **Hypothesised paradigm(s):** HIPAA Security Rule technical safeguards; SLSA / in-toto / sigstore; ITGC / SOC 2 change management; forensic chain of custody.
- **Borrowed clause/concept:** 45 CFR 164.312(b) audit controls and 164.312(c)(1) integrity; in-toto Statement subject digests and signed, typed predicates; SLSA's identified subjects and provenance details; CC8.1's documented and approved change lifecycle; chronological custody, transfer, and integrity records.
- **Analogy-breaking failure mode:** A database calls itself immutable while an authorized writer can omit a change before anchoring, a DBA can roll back both data and local checkpoint, witnesses share the same failure domain, or a partial range is presented as complete. Tamper-evident is not tamper-preventing and does not establish that a semantically fraudulent but valid append is truthful (`INV-005`, `INV-006`, `INV-014` through `INV-018`; ADV-03, ADV-13, ADV-26, ADV-27).
- **Fields required for the analogy:** All common section 7 fields; before/after subject digests, change/action id, ordered event range, explicit boundary positions and completeness scope, inclusion and consistency proofs, checkpoint digest, witness policy and observations, and separate Q1/Q2/Q3 outcomes. Unknown or stale Q3 must remain visible and deny consumers that require tamper evidence (`INV-005` through `INV-007`, `INV-014` through `INV-022`).
- **Validation status:** `internal-hypothesis`.
- **D3 stage-2 question:** Does the target reviewer accept the declared range, witness independence/quorum, checkpoint distribution and retention, and restore/reconciliation procedure as sufficient audit and change-integrity evidence, and which omissions or custody handoffs must be represented?

### `AttributableAuthorship`

- **Hypothesised paradigm(s):** HIPAA Security Rule technical safeguards; SLSA / in-toto / sigstore; forensic chain of custody.
- **Borrowed clause/concept:** 45 CFR 164.312(d) person or entity authentication and 164.312(c)(1) integrity; in-toto Statement `subject` digests and typed predicates; SLSA Provenance's subject and builder/build-process identity concepts; authenticated identity and custody records tied to a uniquely digested item.
- **Analogy-breaking failure mode:** A valid key signature is reported as proof that a named human authored or intended the work despite key sharing, theft, automation, coercion, or missing authority-at-position; mutable author labels are not reconciled to signed evidence; or a later revocation is ignored. The claim cannot provide absolute non-repudiation (`INV-004`, `INV-008` through `INV-013`, `INV-020`; ADV-24, ADV-29).
- **Fields required for the analogy:** All common section 7 fields; signer key/principal and role, authored byte digest and signed context, exact project/action domain, Q1 result, authority-at-position Q2 result, authenticated position and knowledge cut, key lifecycle state, and revocation-with-effect-range result. Any human-identity association must identify its authority source and must not be inferred from a key label (`INV-001` through `INV-004`, `INV-008` through `INV-013`, `INV-019`, `INV-020`).
- **Validation status:** `internal-hypothesis`.
- **D3 stage-2 question:** What identity proofing, key custody, shared/service-account restrictions, revocation response, and corroborating records does the target require before treating this as attributable authorship rather than only key-level authentication?

### `TrustedTimestamp`

- **Hypothesised paradigm(s):** RFC 3161 trusted timestamping; forensic chain of custody.
- **Borrowed clause/concept:** RFC 3161 section 2.1's trusted TSA role and requirements and sections 2.4.1/2.4.2's request/response binding of a message imprint to a policy-qualified timestamp token containing policy, message imprint, serial number, and generation time; chronological custody observations tied to the same subject digest.
- **Analogy-breaking failure mode:** Log order, a caller-supplied timestamp, local server clock, or witness observation is labeled RFC 3161-equivalent without a relying-party-accepted TSA, trust chain, timestamp policy, token validation, algorithm policy, nonce/replay treatment, and validity/revocation evidence. Compromise of the accepted TSA or required time quorum remains a stated limit (`INV-010`; ADV-05, ADV-16, ADV-23, ADV-27).
- **Fields required for the analogy:** All common section 7 fields; subject/message digest, authenticated log position, checkpoint observation time(s), TSA identity and certificate/trust-chain reference, accepted timestamp policy identifier, token digest or RFC 3161 evidence reference, generation time, accuracy/uncertainty when present, nonce/correlation result when used, signature/path/algorithm and revocation validation results, and validation time/cut. The claim must not issue absent accepted TSA/time policy evidence (`INV-010`, `INV-014` through `INV-023`; `TRUST-MODEL.md` section 7).
- **Validation status:** `internal-hypothesis`.
- **D3 stage-2 question:** Which TSA(s), trust anchors, timestamp policies, algorithms, revocation evidence, validation time semantics, retention, and long-term validation procedure would the target accept for this subject class?

### `CapabilityGrant`

- **Hypothesised paradigm(s):** HIPAA Security Rule technical safeguards; ITGC / SOC 2 logical-access and change-management authorization records.
- **Borrowed clause/concept:** 45 CFR 164.312(a)(1) access control and 164.312(d) person or entity authentication; CC8.1's authorization and approval concepts as applied to capability-policy changes. `VERIFY:` The target reviewer must identify its exact logical-access, least-privilege, privileged-access, and SoD criteria; this claim is not asserted to satisfy them merely because it records a grant.
- **Analogy-breaking failure mode:** The grant is treated as enforcement, while the broker fails to authenticate the current caller/harness, trusts a pathname rather than the executable image, permits environment substitution, releases an overbroad credential, or ignores expiry/revocation. A grant signed by a key without grant authority is only authenticated data (`INV-020`, `INV-030` through `INV-034`; ADV-07, ADV-18, ADV-24).
- **Fields required for the analogy:** All common section 7 fields; grantee principal and harness, capability/resource/action scope, executable digest and owner/ACL constraints, safe-resolution requirements, environment constraints, credential class and limits, not-before/expiry positions or policy-selected times, revocation and permission-now status, delegation limits, authorizing principal/role, and enforcement-policy version. Execution must produce a separate broker decision/audit event and fail closed (`INV-010` through `INV-012`, `INV-019` through `INV-023`, `INV-030` through `INV-034`).
- **Validation status:** `internal-hypothesis`.
- **D3 stage-2 question:** Does the target access-control owner accept the grant's identity, authorization, least-privilege, duration, revocation, executable, enforcement, and audit semantics for the proposed resource classes, and what approval or periodic recertification is required?

### `ExternallyAuthenticatedBundle`

- **Hypothesised paradigm(s):** SLSA / in-toto / sigstore; HIPAA Security Rule technical safeguards; forensic chain of custody.
- **Borrowed clause/concept:** in-toto Statement v1's typed statement, `subject` digest set, and predicate; SLSA Provenance v1.0's `buildDefinition` and `runDetails`; Sigstore's bundling of signed material and verification material for offline verification; 45 CFR 164.312(c)(1) integrity and 164.312(e)(1) transmission security; item identity, transfer, and custody continuity.
- **Analogy-breaking failure mode:** A self-signed manifest or detached hash is called externally authenticated; bundle files are omitted or substituted; mutable manifest fields are trusted over authenticated subjects; offline parsing or legacy mode applies weaker semantics; the external signer is not authorized for the bundle type; or the bundle's limited range is presented as complete (`INV-003`, `INV-004`, `INV-006`, `INV-007`, `INV-021`; ADV-24, ADV-29).
- **Fields required for the analogy:** All common section 7 fields; bundle manifest and schema versions, typed predicate or purpose, subject and per-file digests, event count and ranges, completeness and omitted-item counts, external signer identity/role/policy and authority-at-position, signature and verification-material references, inclusion/consistency proofs, anchor/witness observations, parser-limit profile, and allowlisted opaque-item metadata only. Online and offline verification must return equivalent Q1/Q2/Q3 semantics (`INV-001` through `INV-007`, `INV-019` through `INV-023`, `INV-045`, `INV-047`).
- **Validation status:** `internal-hypothesis`.
- **D3 stage-2 question:** Would the target accept this bundle's signer trust, manifest/subject coverage, transfer/custody record, offline verification material, completeness limits, and retention as evidence, and which SLSA level, in-toto layout, Sigstore policy, or local control profile would it require?
